1.1 Information you provide

We collect information about the services you use and how you use them, such as information generated when you use third-party sign-in, the AthliMate App, or other AthliMate products or services. This information includes:

Based on the specific permissions you grant during software installation and/or use, we receive and record information about the device you use, including but not limited to:

When you use features such as sports testing (for example, jump testing and posture assessment) or taking or changing your profile photo, we request camera or photo library permission so that we can collect images and videos that you actively capture or select from your photo library.

## 2. How we use cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small file containing a string of characters that is sent to your computer when you visit a website. When you visit the website again, the cookie allows the website to recognize your browser. Cookies can also store user preferences and other information. You can reset your browser to refuse all cookies or to prompt you when a cookie is sent. However, without cookies, certain website features or services may not work properly.

Cookies and similar technologies are commonly used on the internet. When you use AthliMate App services, we use related technologies to send one or more cookies or anonymous identifiers to your device in order to collect and store information about your visits to and use of the AthliMate App. We treat information collected through cookies and other technologies as non-personal information. However, if local law treats Internet Protocol (IP) addresses or similar identifiers as personal information, we will also treat those identifiers as personal information. We use cookies and similar technologies mainly to provide the following features or services:

2.1 Keeping products and services secure and efficient
We may set authentication and security cookies or anonymous identifiers so that we can confirm whether you have securely signed in to the service, or whether theft, fraud, or other unlawful activity has occurred. These technologies also help us improve service efficiency and sign-in and response speed.

2.2 Helping you access the service more easily
These technologies can save you from repeatedly filling in personal information or entering search content, for example by enabling one-tap sign-in and recording search history.

2.3 Log files
Like most websites, we collect certain information and store it in log files. This information includes Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet service provider (ISP), referring and exit pages, operating system, date and time stamp, and/or clickstream data. We do not associate automatically collected log data with other information we collect.

2.4 Mobile analytics
In the App, we use mobile analytics software to better understand how our mobile software functions on your phone. This software may record information such as how often you use the application, events that occur within the application, cumulative usage, performance data, and where application crashes occur. We do not associate information stored in the analytics software with any personal information you submit in the mobile application.

2.5 Local storage — HTML5/Flash
We use local storage objects (LSOs), such as HTML5 or Flash, to store content. Third parties that work with us to provide certain features on the AthliMate App, or that deliver advertisements based on your browsing behavior, also collect and store related information through HTML5 or Flash cookies. Various browsers provide their own management tools for deleting HTML5 local storage objects.
